## Information processed

The app does not require an account. It stores a local cache of headline metadata returned by GDELT DOC 2.0 using Android app-private SharedPreferences. The app does not request location, contacts, microphone, camera, or notification permissions. It does not include advertising or analytics SDKs in this release.

## Network requests

When the user opens or refreshes the news feed, the app contacts the GDELT DOC 2.0 API. Opening a headline launches the original publisher website in the user's browser. Opening the Economic Calendar launches the official Forex Factory website. GDELT, the publisher, the browser, Forex Factory, and network providers may process connection metadata under their own policies.
